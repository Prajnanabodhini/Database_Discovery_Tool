How to make a meaningful comparison

Choose runs from the same database and compatible discovery modes whenever possible. Mixed databases or missing evidence are allowed but produce warnings. “Compare in browser” keeps results in memory. “Compare and export” explicitly writes HTML, CSV, and JSON files.

Important: ADDED, REMOVED, and CHANGED describe evidence differences only. They do not explain cause, impact, or whether differently named objects are business equivalents.
